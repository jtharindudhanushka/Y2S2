## Course Description & Topics
This course unit covers theoretical foundations, algorithmic methods, and practical frameworks for modern Artificial Intelligence:
1. **Introduction & Prompt Engineering:** Prompt, Context, Harness, and Loop Engineering.
2. **AI Agents & Automation:** Orchestrating workflows and agent tools with n8n.
3. **State Space Search:** Uninformed and informed heuristic search strategies (Uniform Cost Search, A* Search).
4. **Genetic Algorithms:** Chromosome representation, crossover, mutation, fitness evaluation.
5. **Agent Development Kit (ADK):** Google ADK architecture, custom tools, intent routing, and real-world assistant deployment.
6. **Deep Learning & Computer Vision:** Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), convolution layers, pooling, dense classifiers.
7. **Fuzzy Logic Systems:** Fuzzy sets, linguistic variables, membership functions, fuzzification, inference rules, and defuzzification.
8. **Explainable AI (XAI) & Ethics:** Interpretability techniques, regulatory frameworks, and compliance under the EU AI Act.
