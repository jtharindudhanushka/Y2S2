Black boxModern ML prioritises predictive performance over transparency; logic hidden in millions of parameters. Risky in medical diagnosis (life safety), credit scoring (financial loss), autonomous driving (legal liability).
3 goals of XAITrust (legitimate domain logic, not spurious correlations: husky vs snow), Debugging (where / how / why systematic errors on edge cases), Fairness & auditing (no discrimination on protected attributes).
Trade-offGlass-box (linear / logistic regression, decision trees): interpretability very high, power moderate. Ensemble (random forest, XGBoost, LightGBM): moderate / high. Deep learning (CNNs, transformers): low / very high. Use post-hoc methods for the hard ones.
IntrinsicLinear / logistic: coefficient = expected change per unit of a feature (price = 10 + 5 bedrooms + 0.2 area; 3 bed, 100 m² = 45). Decision tree: IF-THEN rules.
Global vs localGlobal: "Which features drive predictions overall?" Local: "Why was this specific loan rejected?"
Permutation importanceShuffle a feature; importance = original score − shuffled score (90 − 62 = 28). Global, any model, weak with correlated features.
LIMELocal Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations: simple model (line) fitted near one point; complex surface looks flat up close. f = x² at 3: slope 6. Valid only locally; varies run to run.
SHAPShapley values (game theory): prediction = prize, features = players, test all coalitions. Baseline 50, Income only 70, Debt only 60, both 90: Income (20 + 30)/2 = 25, Debt (10 + 20)/2 = 15; 25 + 15 = 90 − 50. Consistency and additivity. 2n coalitions.
WarningsExplanation ≠ cause; explanations are approximations; explaining a bad model does not make it good; tailor to the audience.
9 ethical foundationsIntegrity, Originality, Transparency, Accountability, Compliance, Security, Intellectual property, Fairness & bias, Human oversight.
EU AI ActRegulation (EU) 2024/1689 (in force 1 Aug 2024). Risk levels: Unacceptable (banned: social scoring…), High (hiring, credit, education, law enforcement…), Specific transparency / limited (chatbots, deepfakes), Minimal (spam filters, games).
High-risk dutiesRisk management, data governance, technical documentation, logging, transparency, human oversight, accuracy / robustness / cybersecurity.
GPAI and finesGPAI (LLMs): documentation, copyright policy, training-content summary; systemic-risk models extra duties (1025 operations). Fines: €35 M or 7 % (bans); €15 M or 3 %; €7.5 M or 1 %. Dates changing: check official text.
Assignment5-page report: (1) key concerns, (2) how addressed, (3) extra guardrails and why. Brief: classify a system by risk, explainability / transparency duties, compliance and audit workflows. Own words; acknowledge AI use.
Exam tipBe able to explain the black-box problem, the three XAI goals, the trade-off table, global vs local, and compute a permutation drop, a LIME slope and two-feature SHAP values; list the nine ethical foundations and the four EU risk levels with examples.
