TopicRemember
Image generation (course)Create images from scratch or from a prompt; also restoration and scene reconstruction. Models: Stable Diffusion (AI art boom), VAEs, GANs.
Vision tasksClassification (label), object detection (labels + boxes), generation (new pixels).
Lab (course)L = lightness (the grayscale); a, b = colour. Colourise: input L (1×128×128), predict a, b (2×128×128), recombine, convert to RGB.
Pipeline (course)CIFAR-10 photos (labels ignored) → resize 128×128 → RGB→Lab → L/50 − 1, ab/128 → batches of 64. Same scaling must be reversed when rebuilding colour.
U-Net (course)Autoencoder + skip connections. Encoder 64→128→256→512 channels (max-pool halves size), bottleneck 1024 @ 8×8, decoder up-samples (ConvTranspose2d doubles size) and concatenates the matching encoder output; 1×1 conv to 2 channels + tanh. About 31 M parameters.
DoubleConvConv3×3 (pad 1) → BatchNorm → ReLU, twice.
Shapes128×128 → 64 → 32 → 16 → 8 (bottleneck) → back up to 128×128. Skip makes decoder input twice the channels (512 + 512 = 1024).
Training (course)MSE loss, Adam lr 0.001, 18 epochs (15–20 asked), loop: zero grad → forward → loss → backward → step. Checkpoint every epoch; plot loss per epoch.
Personal photo (course)Grayscale → 128×128 → L → predict a, b → recombine → show 3 panels (grayscale input, prediction, restored colour).
Limit (course)Plausible not true colours (jacket red or blue?); MSE averages uncertain colours, so muted.
Diffusion (background)Forward: add noise step by step to a real image until pure noise. Reverse: train a U-Net (loss = MSE on the noise) to remove noise; generate from pure noise by denoising step by step; a text prompt can guide it. Example pixel: 0.8×0.8 + 0.6×0.5 = 0.94.
GAN (background)Generator (random numbers → fake image) vs discriminator (real or fake?): compete and both improve.
VAE (background)Autoencoder with a random latent description; sample a new point and decode; stable but blurry.
Exam tipKnow the Lab setup and shapes, describe the U-Net (encoder, bottleneck, decoder, skip connections), the loss and training loop, and the limits. For diffusion, GANs and VAEs give a one-line idea each; your materials only name them.
