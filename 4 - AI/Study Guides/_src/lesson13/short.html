Short note: revision sheet

TopicRemember
WhatTechnique to include vague human assessments in computing: decision making, identification, pattern recognition, optimisation, control. Zadeh, 1965. No accurate model needed; linguistic variables; faster, simpler controllers; decision support for managers.
Boolean vs fuzzy"Is it cold?" Yes/1, No/0 vs Very much 0.9, Little 0.25, Very less 0.1. Tip rules: poor/bad → cheap, good → average, excellent/delicious → generous (non-linear surface).
ApplicationsSendai Subway 1987: controls acceleration, deceleration, braking; 10 % less energy; smooth ride. Air conditioners (cool / heat / no change).
Crisp vs fuzzy setCrisp: member or not (0/1). Fuzzy: partial membership; μX(y): X → [0, 1]. Colour with 0.38 Green. μB = 1/x1 + 1/x2 + 1/x3 + 0.7/x4 + 0.3/x5. Degree is not a probability.
TermsFuzzy variable (fuzzy sets as values); linguistic variable (word values); linguistic hedge (very, somewhat, extremely; very = μ²).
Fuzzy propositionsMembership = truth value: 0 absolute falsity, 1 absolute truth; "Joe is short" = μshort(Joe). Classical laws (De Morgan, contraposition…) listed on slides; A OR NOT A is not always 1 in fuzzy logic.
OperationsUnion max; intersection min; complement 1 − μ. (0.6, 0.8): AND 0.6, OR 0.8, NOT 0.4.
RulesIf antecedent(s) Then consequent(s). Antecedents combine fuzzy sets with logic operators; inputs fuzzified first; consequent usually one fuzzy set.
SystemCrisp input → Fuzzifier → Inference (fuzzy sets + fuzzy rules) → Defuzzifier → crisp output. Rule table (fuzzy associative matrix) prepared by experts; can have N variables.
Membership functionsTriangular (a, m, b): (x−a)/(m−a), (b−x)/(b−m). Trapezoidal (a, b, c, d): flat 1 on b..c. L / R shoulders. Gaussian exp(−(x−m)²/2k²). Singleton. Piecewise linear. Triangle (20,25,30) at 23 = 0.6.
Fuzzify36 °F: 0.7 Freezing, 0.3 Cool (no threshold). 28 °C with warm tri(20,25,30), hot 25→30: warm 0.4, hot 0.6.
InferenceMamdani and Sugeno (differ in output). Antecedent: AND = min, OR = max (service 3, food 8: 0.0 OR 0.7 = 0.7). Implication: min truncates the consequent (or product scales). Aggregation: max, sum or probabilistic sum.
DefuzzifyFirst / middle / last / mean of maximum; centre of gravity COG = Σ μx / Σ μ (integrals if continuous). Restaurant tip 16.7 %. (0.2, 0.5, 0.3 at 20, 50, 80) = 53.
SugenoConstant outputs: Σ αz / Σ α. (0.4 × 50 + 0.6 × 80)/1 = 68.
Fan at 28 °CClip medium 0.4, high 0.6; combine (max); centroid 264 / 3.6 = 73.3.
Heater (35 °F, 1 °F/h)Rules: (LOW, LOW) FAST 0.6; (MED, MED) SLOW 0.4; (LOW, MED) FAST 0.4; (MED, LOW) MEDIUM 0.8 (strength = min). y = (0.6×800 + 0.4×300 + 0.4×800 + 0.8×500)/2.2 = 600 R/M.
Exam tipBe ready to compute a triangular / trapezoidal membership value, AND / OR / NOT of two degrees, rule strengths, a clipped aggregate and a centre-of-gravity (weighted-average) output, and to explain crisp vs fuzzy, linguistic variables and the four system components.
