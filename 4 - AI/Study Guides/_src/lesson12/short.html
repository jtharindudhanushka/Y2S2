TopicRemember
SI (course)Swarm Intelligence = behaviour inspired by ants, birds, etc. One of the classical / complementary AI techniques: GA, SI, FL, RB (and ML). Can be hybridised (e.g. LCS = GA + ML).
Outline (course)Item 9 of the course outline, after fuzzy logic and before RL basics. PSO and ACO appear on the meta-heuristic map in the GA slides.
Swarm idea (background)Many simple agents, no leader, local rules, indirect communication; the group solves a hard problem. Population-based like a GA but agents learn from each other instead of selection / crossover.
ACO (background)Pheromone trails; shorter path gets pheromone faster (positive feedback); evaporation forgets poor paths. P(path) = pheromone / total. τ ← (1 − ρ)τ + deposit (Q / L). Example: 4 vs 1 → P(short) 0.8; after update 8 vs 1.5 → 0.84. Good for routes (TSP).
PSO (background)Particles with position x and velocity v; remember pbest, know gbest. vnew = w v + c1r1(pbest − x) + c2r2(gbest − x); xnew = x + vnew. Example: x = 4, v = 1, pbest 3, gbest 1: vnew = −2.8, xnew = 1.2 (f: 16 → 1.44). Good for continuous problems.
Exam tipIf asked, say SI is nature-inspired optimisation based on collective behaviour of ants and birds, and place it next to GA and fuzzy logic on the AI map. The ACO and PSO formulas are background, not from your lecturer.
