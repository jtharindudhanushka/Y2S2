TopicRemember
Discriminative / predictive AI (course)Classifies / predicts from input: spam detection, face recognition, churn, house price. Tasks: classification, regression, time-series forecasting.
Generative AI (course)Creates new text, images, audio, video, code: ChatGPT, music generation. "Gen AI". Examiner (discriminative) vs artist (generative).
Generative ML (course)Generates new content or sequences from learned patterns, e.g. next-word prediction. Generative ML = unsupervised + supervised fine-tuning + reinforcement learning.
Caution (course)Supervised / unsupervised = how it learns; predictive / generative = what it produces.
AI map (course)AI ⊃ ML ⊃ neural networks ⊃ deep NNs ⊃ transformers (ChatGPT). GA, SI, FL are also inside AI.
Transformer (course)Deep learning model that reads all the words together and decides which words matter to each other. Architecture behind modern LLMs (ChatGPT). Can have billions of weights (hard to explain).
Generation (course)"ChatGPT writes text one word at a time": predict next word, append, repeat.
Why not RNN / LSTMRNN: one step at a time, vanishing / exploding gradients (wn). LSTM: gated long-term lane but still sequential. Transformer: every word looks at every other directly, in parallel.
Token, embedding (background)Token = piece of text; embedding = its vector of numbers (learned); position information added; context window = max tokens seen.
Self-attention (background)Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(QKT/√d) V. Scores → softmax weights (add to 1) → weighted sum of values. Example: C = (1,1) with A = (1,0), B = (0,1): weights 0.248, 0.248, 0.503; new C = (0.752, 0.752). Multi-head = several attentions in parallel.
Block (background)Self-attention → add & norm → feed-forward → add & norm; stack N blocks; last layer softmax over vocabulary gives next-token probabilities (e.g. 0.58, 0.21, 0.13, 0.08).
LLM / GPAI (background + course)Large transformer trained on lots of text; EU AI Act calls such models general-purpose AI (GPAI). Can hallucinate: RAG gives it facts.
Training (course)1 unsupervised (learn patterns; next word is its own label) → 2 supervised fine-tuning (good example answers) → 3 reinforcement learning (rewards). Same backprop + gradient descent machinery as Lesson 6.
Exam tipBe able to contrast discriminative and generative AI with examples, state the lecturer's recipe (unsupervised + supervised fine-tuning + reinforcement learning), place transformers on the AI map and describe in one sentence what a transformer does. The attention arithmetic is background: learn it for understanding rather than as a likely calculation.
