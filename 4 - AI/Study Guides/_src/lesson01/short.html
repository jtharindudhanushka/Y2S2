Short note: revision sheet

TopicRemember
AIScience of developing techniques to generate intelligence artificially (machines that reason, learn, perceive, decide). Disruptive technology: replaces older methods and reshapes industries.
ANI / AGI / ASINarrow (one task; weak AI; exists: Siri, Maps, face recognition) · General (any intellectual task; strong AI; hypothetical) · Super (beats humans at everything; future).
Strong vs weak AIStrong: truly thinks like a human brain (chatbots, autonomous vehicles, as in the notes). Weak: pretends / performs a function (disease detection, recommenders).
HistoryWWII (Bombe, Enigma, Colossus, ENIAC) · 1943 artificial neuron · 1948 Cybernetics · 1950 Turing Test · 1956 Dartmouth: "AI" (McCarthy), Minsky · 1959 "Machine Learning" (Samuel).
Turing TestInterrogator asks written questions to a hidden human and machine; if it cannot tell the machine apart, the machine is intelligent. Tests behaviour.
Chinese Room (Searle)Rule book + mapping function gives perfect answers without understanding: a machine can act intelligent without being intelligent.
GOFAIRule-based / symbolic; facts and rules with symbols; Prolog, LISP; expert systems.
Data-drivenLearn patterns from data = machine learning. Works today thanks to big data + GPU / TPU / cloud.
3 Vs (Laney 2001)Volume, Velocity, Variety.
Train / testBuild on training data, judge on unseen testing data. Perfect training + bad testing = overfitting.
AI mapAI ⊃ ML ⊃ neural networks ⊃ deep learning ⊃ transformers. Alongside: GA, swarm, fuzzy logic, rule-based. Hybrids: LCS.
Predictive vs generativeDiscriminative / predictive: classify or predict (spam, face recognition; classification, regression, forecasting). Generative: create new content (ChatGPT, music).
Sense → actSensors collect, intelligent system decides, actuators act. Embodied (car, humanoid) vs disembodied (ChatGPT, Translate).
4 engineering skillsPrompt (the order) · Context (recipe book / RAG) · Harness (safety gear, sandbox, tools) · Loop (quality-control cycle).
MarkingPaper 70 %, assignments 30 %.
Exam tipKnow the three levels with an example each, the two approaches (rules vs data) with their tools, and the 1950 / 1956 / 1959 dates with who is attached to each.
