# Assignment: EU AI Act Case Analysis

**Course:** INTE 22303 - Artificial Intelligence

### Objective
Analyze real-world AI applications against the regulatory framework of the European Union AI Act.

### Key Tasks
1. Classify the given system into AI Act risk categories: Unacceptable Risk, High Risk, Specific Transparency Risk, or Minimal Risk.
2. Detail transparency and explainability obligations.
3. Propose compliance mechanisms and algorithmic audit workflows.
