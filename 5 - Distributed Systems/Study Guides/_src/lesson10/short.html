Common mistakes

MistakeFix
Writing a list of words without explanationOne explained point per mark, with an example.
"Compare" answered as two separate paragraphsUse a table with the same rows for both sides.
No diagram for an architecture questionDraw and label it; it earns marks and saves words.
Confusing service and deployment modelsIaaS/PaaS/SaaS = what; public/private/hybrid = who owns/uses.
Saying CA is a good choice for big distributed systemsPartitions happen; choose C or A.
2PC "blocks when a participant says NO"It aborts; it blocks when the coordinator fails after YES votes.
Mixing RTO and RPO; vMotion and Storage vMotion; image and container; edge and fogLearn the Part A definitions word for word.
