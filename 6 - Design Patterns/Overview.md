- **Course Code:** INTE 22313
- **Course Title:** Software Design Patterns & Frameworks
- **Department:** Department of Industrial Management, University of Kelaniya

---

## Course Topics Covered
1. **Introduction to Design Patterns:** Gang of Four (GoF) classifications, design principles, benefits and trade-offs.
2. **Creational Patterns:**
   - Singleton Pattern (Thread-safety, lazy initialization, double-checked locking).
   - Factory Method Pattern (Decoupling object creation, creator vs concrete creators).
3. **Structural Patterns:**
   - Facade Pattern (Simplified unified interface to complex subsystems).
   - Adapter Pattern (Interface incompatible classes conversion).
   - Proxy Pattern (Surrogate/placeholder for lazy loading, access control, virtual proxies).
   - Decorator Pattern (Dynamic behavior extension adhering to open/closed principle).
4. **Behavioral Patterns:**
   - Template Method Pattern (Algorithm skeleton in superclass, steps in subclasses).
   - Chain of Responsibility (Decoupling sender and receivers through a processing chain).
   - State Pattern (Altering behavior when internal state changes).
5. **Architectural Patterns & Best Practices:**
   - Case Studies & Microservices Architecture.
   - SOLID Design Principles (Single Responsibility, Open/Closed, Liskov Substitution, Interface Segregation, Dependency Inversion).
