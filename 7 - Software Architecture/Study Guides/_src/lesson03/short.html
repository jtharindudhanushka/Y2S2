Problem vs solutionProblem framing and solution-oriented work overlap in systems thinking + design thinking (SDLC context).
Systems thinkingLook beyond surface "fixes" to the network of underlying causes and effects (traditional thinking = simple cause → effect).
IcebergEvents (what happened?) → Patterns (what is happening over time?) → Structures (what enables the pattern?) → Mental models. Down = analyse and understand; up = innovate and transform.
Time viewSystems thinking = what was; design thinking = what can be; overlap = what is.
Design thinking mindsets (10)Curiosity · focus on people · accept complexity · visualise and show · experiment and iterate · co-create, grow, scale · varying mental states · process awareness · networked collaboration · reflect on actions.
Right teamCustomer need · interdisciplinary team · right mindset · leeway for experimentation · creativity · courage to question.
3 things importantKeep your personality · love it, change it or leave it · don't do it alone.
Diverge / convergeDiverge: identify, discuss, different perspectives. Converge: prioritise, select, refine. Groan zone in between, then point of decision.
Double diamondThink like a system: Discover, Define (problem definition). Act like an entrepreneur: Develop, Deliver (prototyping, selection, mentoring).
Exam tipBe ready to explain systems thinking with the iceberg and to apply it to a software failure; and to name the four double-diamond phases with their activities.
