Short note: revision sheet

TopicRemember
Embedded systemDedicated computer inside another system: sensors → processor (firmware) → actuators.
ConstraintsCost, weight/size, power, real-time.
Why softwareEasier to implement, cheaper, easier to debug and modify.
MPU vs MCUMPU: separate chips, single board, fast, costly, hot. MCU: one chip, single chip computer, cheap, low power, compact.
MCU partsCPU, ROM(Flash, firmware), RAM(variables), EEPROM(settings), ADC, OSC, PWM, timers, I/O ports, USART/SPI/I2C, RESET.
SerialUSART to PC; SPI fast 4 wires; I2C (TWI) slower 2 wires.
ProgrammingSource → compiler → HEX → programmer/bootloader → Flash. Endless loop needed (no OS).
ATmega328PHarvard RISC, 8-bit, 20 MHz max (Uno 16 MHz), 32 KB Flash, 2 KB RAM, 1 KB EEPROM, 23 I/O pins, 3 timers, 6 PWM, 6 ch 10-bit ADC, 1 USART, 2 SPI, 1 I2C, 28-pin PDIP.
Arduino UnoPins 0 to 13 digital (0,1 = Rx,Tx), PWM on 3,5,6,9,10,11, A0 to A5 = ADC, pin 13 LED = PB5. 5 V regulated, jack below 12 V.
Pin mapD0 to D7 = PD0 to PD7; D8 to D13 = PB0 to PB5; A0 to A5 = PC0 to PC5.
I/O pinPort register bit = logic level; high about 2 to 5 V, low about 0 to 1 V.
