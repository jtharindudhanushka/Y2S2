TransducerEnergy form converter at the boundary: sensors (in) and actuators (out).
Digital sensorsReed switch (NO, closes in magnetic field), Hall switch AH1815 (omnipolar, open drain, low = magnet), PIR (pyroelectric infrared, motion), flame (IR). Read with a digital pin.
Analog sensorsWater level, accelerometer ADXL335 (3 outputs, ±3 g, 300 mV/g at 3 V), joystick (2 outputs), LDR, LM35. Read with ADC.
Analog + digitalSound, soil moisture, gas/smoke modules.
ActuatorsLED/RGB, bulb, motor, solenoid, thermoelectric cooler, relay. Pin about 20 mA only, so use a driver.
BJT driver (BC547)NPN, 45 V, 100 mA. RB = (5 − 0.7)/IB with IB ≈ IC/10. Emitter to GND. Flyback diode on inductive loads.
MOSFET driver (2N7000)N-channel, 60 V, 200 mA, VGS(th) 0.8 to 3 V. Gate ~100 Ω + 10 kΩ pull-down to GND.
Flyback diode1N4001 across coil: cathode to +V, anode to collector/drain. Absorbs the turn-off spike.
RelayCoil via transistor + flyback diode; COM/NO/NC contacts isolate the load; mains only with qualified supervision.
